## Design guidance

- In the hypothesis, explain the expected cooling, fan, pump and total EUI
  effect for the Hong Kong climate, and how daylight, view and the 30%
  projection cap remain protected.
- Begin with broad exploration across the available window categories and
  shading categories.
- Treat window type, WWR and shading as per-facade variables; do not assume
  one global setting before the evidence supports it.
- Do not copy an earlier proposal unchanged.
