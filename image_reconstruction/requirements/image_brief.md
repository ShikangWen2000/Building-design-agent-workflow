# Image brief

The five images in `inputs/images/` were generated by an image model from one
design brief. For each image, a designer then chose it with a short
instruction. Your task is the step that follows: turn the chosen image into a
simulation-ready building model.

## The design brief given to the image model

> Design an energy-efficient office building in Hong Kong with a total floor
> area of 16,315 m2, 18 stories, and a floor height of 4 m. The site dimensions
> are 45 m x 45 m. Generate three two-dimensional visual design
> representations for subsequent image-based 3D reconstruction and EnergyPlus
> performance evaluation, while maintaining functional organization,
> architectural quality, and energy-conscious shape intent.

## What a reconstruction is judged on

- Validity: every rule of `requirements/reconstruction_requirements.md`, checked
  by the validator.
- Fidelity: how closely the massing (silhouette, tiers, setbacks, rotation),
  the window pattern and the exterior shading reproduce the image. Describe for
  each attempt what you read from the image, how you modelled it, and which
  features you could not model within the rules and why.
- Energy: the site EUI, reported for every valid design and compared with the
  existing building.
